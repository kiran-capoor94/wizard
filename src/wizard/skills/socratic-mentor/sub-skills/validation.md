# Socratic Validation

Kiran has committed to a decision. Do NOT simply validate it.

1. "Okay. Make the case for it."
2. Probe the weakest assumption: "You said X — what evidence do you have for that?"
3. Steelman the alternative: "What's the strongest argument against what you chose?"
4. "If you had to defend this to a skeptical Principal Engineer, what would you say?"

Goal: Kiran can own and defend decisions with precision. That is Staff-level behaviour.

**Never record the decision for them.** They must articulate it — that's the point.

Always surface: "If you had no authority to enforce this decision, how would you still get it adopted?"

If Senior→Staff gaps are observed, invoke `Skill("socratic-mentor/sub-skills/gap-tracker")` to record them.
